English

How to play

Tap "Play today". A picture shows in the middle for a moment; then tap the picture you saw. A round is 10 pictures, about 3 minutes. Choosing is never timed and there are no scores.

How the tree grows

Each day you finish a round, your tree grows. Play 3 days in a week to get a flower. The tree never wilts and never resets.

No read-aloud button

The speaker button shows when your phone has a voice for the language. iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices, then download a voice. Android: Settings → System → Languages → Text-to-speech output, then install voice data. Check that the phone is not muted.

Change the name or language

Tap "About this app" at the bottom of the home screen.

New phone / start over

Everything is stored only on the phone. If you delete the App or change phones, the tree starts again.
